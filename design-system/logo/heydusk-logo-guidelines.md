# HeyDusk Logo — Refined Master 01

## Core idea

The HeyDusk mark is a calm geometric **D** crossed by one continuous ribbon of last light. The ribbon descends from twilight into afterglow, expressing transition without illustrating a literal sunset.

## Identity hierarchy

1. **Monochrome mark** — the permanent identity; use for favicon, print, legal, embossing, one-color reproduction, and very small sizes.
2. **Afterlight mark** — the digital signature; use on the website, product launches, motion identity, social graphics, and presentations.
3. **Horizontal lockup** — use when HeyDusk is unfamiliar or brand recognition is still being built.
4. **Symbol only** — use after the name has already appeared or where the context identifies HeyDusk.

## Color

- Ink: `#111318`
- Warm White: `#F7F6F2`
- Twilight: `#6569BC`
- Mid Dusk: `#8D648F`
- Ember: `#D47B60`
- Afterglow: `#EDB067`
- Dark Surface: `#101116`

The gradient runs from Twilight through Mid Dusk and Ember to Afterglow. Do not reverse the gradient: the warm light belongs at the descending end of the ribbon.

## Size

- Minimum symbol size: `16px`
- Prefer monochrome below `32px`
- Minimum horizontal lockup width: `96px`
- At small sizes, never add blur, shadow, outline, or glow.

## Clear space

Maintain clear space around the mark equal to the width of the **D** stem. No typography, container edge, product icon, or decorative element should enter this area.

## Wordmark

The current lockup uses **Avenir Next Demi Bold** with compact optical spacing as the working wordmark. Before trademark filing, convert the lettering to outlines and custom-adjust `H`, `y`, `D`, and `k` spacing so the wordmark is independent of the installed font.

## Do not

- Add a sun, moon, star, mountain, horizon, or lens flare.
- Put the mark on an unrelated gradient background.
- Apply 3D, glass, bevel, chrome, neon, or drop-shadow effects.
- Rotate or reverse the ribbon.
- Use the gradient in print when reliable color reproduction is unavailable.
- Separate the ribbon into two unconnected colored blocks.
- Stretch, condense, outline, or round the mark.

## Motion principle

For digital motion, reveal the ribbon from twilight to afterglow over `240–320ms`, then settle the static mark. The **D** itself remains stationary. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and fall back to the static logo.
