# Home Page Override

## Dusk Field Hero

- Preserve the Swiss editorial grid, but let one original cinematic image carry the emotional layer.
- Place the visual in the right 60–70% of the hero. Keep the left side quiet for the headline.
- Dissolve every image edge into `--paper` with masks or overlays; never show a rectangular photo frame.
- The sun or last line of light is the single focal point. Avoid decorative gradients, glass panels, or multiple competing objects.
- Keep image motion limited to a slow 6–10px drift and disable it with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Header

- Group navigation and contact action on the right, leaving the brand isolated on the left.
- Navigation uses a restrained translucent capsule with a hairline border; the contact action uses the inverse ink/paper treatment.
- All interactive targets remain at least 44px and keep visible focus states.

## Hero Typography

- Maximum two intentional lines on desktop.
- Use a calmer line-height around `0.98` and letter-spacing around `-0.052em`; never compress display type to the point that counters or word shapes feel crowded.
- On mobile, allow the title to reflow naturally and keep it visually separate from the image focal point.
